- Compliance posture: Strictly additive warning under PUWER 1998 Reg 24 and IEC 60204-1; zero interference with interlock stop chains.
- Information architecture: Clean executive 3-card summary with in-depth technical data tucked under collapsible details drawers.

## Landmines
- Series wiring with door stop loops or photocells: breaks PUWER 1998 compliance and invalidates door manufacturer warranty.
- Continuous alarm tones: leads to 100 percent worker habituation within shifts; pulse duration must not exceed 3 seconds.
- Over-engineered cloud or microcontroller dependencies: industrial door maintenance engineers reject systems requiring software configuration.
