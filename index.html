HUMAN FACTORS

Why 2.5-Second Auto-Stop Works

Continuous reverse beepers cause complete sensory gating: workers report hearing nothing after two days of exposure. A sharp 2.5-second burst triggers the orienting reflex, halting pedestrian stride before the threshold, then mutes so personnel working near the bay are not subjected to chronic noise pollution.

[LAW-02] Statutory Legal Brief: PUWER 1998 & IEC 60204-1 Fail-Safe Architecture HSE Safety Duty, Additive Integration, UKCA Pathway [+] Expand
ENGINEERING COMPLIANCE MEMO

Statutory Positioning: PUWER 1998 & IEC 60204-1

REF: BD-PUWER-2026-UK

1. Additive vs Subtractive Safety Function:
Under the Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations 1998 (PUWER), alterations to work equipment must not compromise intrinsic machine safety. The BayDoor SDR-24 module is strictly additive: it monitors existing potential-free auxiliary contacts or the 24VDC motor contactor coil in parallel. It does not wire in series with emergency stops, bottom optical safety edges (DW switches), or photocell safety beams.

2. PUWER Regulation 24 (Warning Devices):
Places a statutory duty on dutyholders to ensure equipment incorporates appropriate warning equipment. BayDoor satisfies this requirement by replacing fallible human compliance (driver honking) with deterministic, automated optical and audible alerts.

3. IEC 60204-1 Clause 9.4 (Failure Protection):
In the event of wire severance, internal component short-circuit, or module power loss, the BayDoor module defaults to a de-asserted, silent state. The door controller's primary safety circuitry operates with complete independence across a 3.75kV galvanic boundary.

4. CE / UKCA Self-Declaration Pathway:
Because operating voltage is 24VDC (SELV), the system is exempt from the Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU. CE and UKCA conformity are maintained via direct self-declaration under the EMC Directive 2014/30/EU (EN 61000-6-2 immunity, EN 61000-6-4 emissions) and RoHS 2011/65/EU.
